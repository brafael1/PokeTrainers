# Credits & Licensing

The trainer sprites in `data/trainers/` (1,500 PNGs) come from
[Pokemon Showdown](https://play.pokemonshowdown.com/sprites/trainers/), the free,
open-source battle simulator by the Smogon community.

The Pokemon Showdown trainer sprites page states:

> Many of these sprites are not from the games. Credit must be given to the
> appropriate artist if used elsewhere. DO NOT EDIT without permission.

So, before using these sprites in a public project, please credit the artists
and ask permission before editing them.

## License notes

- The **code** in this repository is MIT licensed (see `LICENSE`).
- The **sprites** are NOT covered by the MIT license of this project.
  - Sprites ripped from official games belong to Nintendo / Game Freak /
    The Pokemon Company.
  - Community-made sprites (many of the trainers here) are the work of the
    artists listed below, and their license is determined separately by the
    Pokemon Showdown project.
- Pokemon and Pokemon character names are trademarks of Nintendo.
